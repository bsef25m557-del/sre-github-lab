# punjab mass transit system 
## Overview 
 . The app covers four services: the Orange Line train, Metro Bus, Speedo Bus and Electro Bus.
Today, each service works in its own way. Passengers use different payment methods and have little live information. Staff depend on registers and phone calls. The app will bring all four services into one system.
The app has a separate section for each service. A passenger chooses a section, then he can perform the section specific operations and tracks that particular vehicle. Staff log in and see only the screen for their own service and job. For example, a Speedo bus warden sees only Speedo related functionalities.

## Scope
    ##High Level Features
	Four service sections: Orange Line, Metro Bus, Speedo Bus and Electro Bus, each with its own routes, timings and fares.•	Payment: Passengers pay digitally through mobile wallets or cards, and get e-tickets and a trip history
	Live tracking: Passengers see vehicles on a map with estimated arrival times, and get delay and cancellation alerts.
	Role-based staff interfaces: 
	Drivers see their schedule, alerts and breakdown reporting.
	Wardens scan tickets and report incidents.
	Station managers see live vehicles, complaints and daily reports.
	Director dashboard: Reports on ridership, revenue and delays for each service and for all four together.
## Out of Scope / Exclusions
	Private taxi services such as Bykea, Yango and inDrive.
	Other transport such as rickshaws, private vans and intercity buses.
	Bus or trains hardware related facilities are explicitly excluded.
	Staff salary and payroll.

## Stakeholders
	Passengers: They want easy payment, live tracking, clear routes and fares, and delay alerts. They also want help and safety for women, elderly people and disabled passengers.
	Station Manager: They want a live view of vehicles at the station. They also want incident and complaint records and automatic daily reports.
	Punjab Mass Transit Director: They want dashboards and reports for ridership, revenue, delays and complaints across all four services, to help with planning.
	Bus/Train Warden: They want a simple mobile screen to scan tickets, report incidents and contact the driver and station.

## Functional requirements
 -the system shall let passengers track the bus
 -the system shall let passengers pay bus ticket online
 -the system shall let punjab mass transit authority to track their buses and drivers  
## Nonfunctional requirements
 -the app must be available 99% of the time
 -the gps system must be 99% accurate
 -the passengers can open the tracking gps within 3 seconds after login


## success criteria 
The project is successful if, within one year of launch:
	-At least 100,000 passengers register and use the app.
	-At least 40% of trips on covered routes are paid through the app.
	-At least 90% of vehicles show live position with an arrival time error under 3 minutes.
	-The app rating is 4.0 out of 5 or better.
	-Incident response time is reduced by at least 30%.
	-Station managers produce the daily report in under 10 minutes.
	-The director's dashboard covers all four services.
	-The system is available at least 99% of the time during service hours.
